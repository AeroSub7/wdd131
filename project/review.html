AeroSub Satisfactory

Steam Review

This is my review that I put on steam on Dec 3, 2024 with 996 hrs: Love this game. Building and organizing your production, Then blood pumping combat to spice things up make it a quite enjoyable single player experience. Multiplayer is fun but can get lagging in 100 plus hour game, depending on computer. Also if combat is not your thing you can change in setting to make creatures passive. This is compared to some other factory games but I do like that factory building is at your pace and you are in control of your progression the most.

I can now say that the multiplayer is much better with the full release of the game. The pictures that you see in the heading is a time-lapse of the one time I beat the game. If you refresh the page, it will progress the time-lapse. You can see how my factory progressed after I have played about 900 hours. Don't worry if your factory is as organized as mine because everyone can play their own way. And to be honest it is not that organized or pretty.

Last Thoughts

One thing I love about Satisfactory is the visuals. Every once in while I just pause to take in the environment. There are other factory games out there, but Satisfactory is my favorite.
