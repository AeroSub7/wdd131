AeroSub Satisfactory

Experience

I started playing Satisfactory when it came out on the Epic Game Store. I do not know how I found out about it(Most likely a Youtuber), but I really liked it as soon as I played it.

One thing I like most is that you can play with other people. I will be honest and say that when I joined a community server with some friends that I may have gone overboard with building and progressing up the tech tree. I did play with my brother SilentDemise through to the end of the game when it was release fully.

I started many different game saves of Satisfactory. Some was I did not want to tare down the chaos and reorganize my current factory. I also tried different ways to play the game like challenges and different starting positions. I even tried speed running to the first tier for a little bit. My brother Forcerun even helped me out with it some at a Lanfest party.

I believe that my experience with Satisfactory has been a positive influence in my life. Some sayings from it like "We do not waste" still stick out to me. So "Stay Productive" and I hope you can give it a try at least once.
